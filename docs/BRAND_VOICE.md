# İLLURÊ BRAND VOICE GUIDE

> "Familiar notes. Our point of view."

---

## 1. The Core Character of İLLURÊ

İLLURÊ is a contemporary Indian fragrance label creating concentrated, high-character interpretations of iconic scent profiles. We speak like a creative studio or a modern fashion campaign — confident, concise, visual, and grounded.

### We are:
- **Confident & Direct**: Say what the scent does in 8 words, not 80.
- **Understated & Modern**: High standards speak for themselves. We do not plead for legitimacy.
- **Sensual without Fluff**: Focus on skin, temperature, memory, tension, and warmth.
- **A Little Sharp / Playful**: A subtle edge ("You don't need an occasion", "Wear something they remember").

### We are NEVER:
- **AI Luxury Slop**: "Exquisite tapestry of timeless elegance", "Embark on an unforgettable sensory journey".
- **Fake French Heritage**: Faux aristocratic claims, French terms sprinkled randomly to sound expensive.
- **Over-poetic or Ponderous**: Every sentence does not need to be a gothic prose poem.
- **Corporate or Defensive**: No generic SaaS copy, no fake lab claims.

## 3. Strict Word Blacklist for Customer-Facing Copy

Do NOT use repeatedly:
- *Haute Parfumerie*
- *Olfactory Universe*
- *Bespoke*
- *Exquisite*
- *Timeless Elegance*
- *Uncompromising Luxury*
- *Elevated Experience*
- *Crafted to Perfection*
- *Meticulously Crafted*
- *Sensory Journey*
- *Embark on a Journey*
- *Discover the Art of*
- *Masterpiece*
- *Opulent*
- *Sophisticated Elegance*
- *Unparalleled*
- *Private Notes*
- *Atelier / Maison*
- *Curated*
- *Immersive*

---

## 4. The Human Read Test

Before publishing any customer-facing sentence, pass it through two checks:
1. **"Would a real brand put this on Instagram?"** (If no, rewrite it.)
2. **"Could this sentence appear on 500 AI-generated luxury websites?"** (If yes, rewrite it.)
